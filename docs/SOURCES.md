## Phytoplankton — Patagonian Shelf blooms

NASA Earthdata · Ocean color & chlorophyll:
https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/topics/ocean/ocean-color

Carbon pathway:
https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/topics/ocean/ocean-color

**Map layer:** Surface chlorophyll-a

Provider: NASA GIBS / Aqua MODIS

Edition: Daily observation · choose date

Method: Satellite pigment proxy

Near-surface chlorophyll-a in mg/m³. Clouds and missing overpasses create gaps. Pigment concentration is not a species census or carbon credit estimate.

Endpoint:
https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/wms/epsg3857/best/wms.cgi

Dataset information:
